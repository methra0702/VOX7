# VOX7 — Your Voice. Your Intelligence.

**Project:** VOX7 – A Multilingual AI Voice Companion  
**Challenge:** The Great Indian AI Internship Challenge

### Problem
Many people find text-based AI interfaces less natural or accessible, particularly when they are more comfortable speaking in Indian languages or switching between languages. VOX7 aims to make interacting with AI easier through voice.

### Our Solution
VOX7 is a multilingual AI voice companion that allows users to speak naturally and receive intelligent spoken responses. It is designed to support the languages available through Gnani AI's speech services, including multilingual and code-mixed conversations.

### How It Works
1. The user records a voice message through VOX7's Voice Studio.
2. **Gnani Prisma** converts the speech into text.
3. **Google Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite** generates an intelligent response.
4. **Gnani Timbre** converts the response into spoken audio.
5. The user can listen to the response and explore previous conversations.

### Key Features
- Multilingual voice interaction using Gnani speech services.
- Speech-to-text and text-to-speech integration.
- AI-powered conversational responses.
- Interactive AI companion, VOXIE, with playful reactions.
- Dance and singing-style interactions.
- Conversation interface for reviewing previous interactions.

### Technology Used
- **Gnani AI Prisma:** Speech recognition.
- **Gnani AI Timbre:** Speech synthesis.
- **Google Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite:** AI response generation.
- **Python and FastAPI:** Backend.
- **React and Vite:** Frontend.

### Impact
VOX7 aims to make AI interaction more natural, engaging, and accessible by bringing voice-first conversations and multilingual support into one experience.
